For a list of numbers, the arithmetic mean is the sum divided by the number of entries. The median is the middle value after sorting. These two statistics answer different questions: the mean includes every observation, while the median is less sensitive to unusually large or small values. Neither statistic replaces the original measurements, especially when the sample is small.
